## 2. What the product is

**A real dried heirloom "glass gem"-type corn cob segment, stabilized and lacquered, turned into a jewelry charm.**

- **Body:** a short cob section, ~6–9 cm long × ~3–3.5 cm wide (fits in a palm; see P0144, P0177). Both ends are trimmed flat/rounded.
  ~12–16 kernel rows, kernels are plump rounded rondelles (bead-like), tightly packed in straight-to-slightly-spiral rows.
- **Finish:** glossy resin/lacquer coat — kernels read like polished jade, agate or opal. Some colorways show
  **radial "starburst" streaks** inside each kernel (Peach/Orange & Amber: P0048, P0070) — the hallmark detail.
  Others are milky/opalescent (Light Pink, Lavender) or near-opaque glossy (Black: P0189).
- **Hardware ("cap"):** gold-tone (sometimes silver) filigree bead cap or crown on one end, with a jump ring +
  **lobster clasp** — so it clips onto a bag, keys, or a strap. Cap styles seen: 6-prong crown, flower/scalloped filigree,
  rhinestone crown (P0001, P0189). Cap_Details folders = end-on shots of the cob showing kernel spiral + cap.
- **Bundle:** almost every shot pairs the cob with a **coordinated beaded strap/bracelet** (aquamarine-style blue beads
  with AB crystal cubes & gold clover spacer; pearls with gold; crackle glass; rhodonite/black onyx; tassels). It's
  sold as a **bag charm / keychain set**, not a hanging tree ornament.
- **Round_Ornaments (03_Peach_Orange):** a spherical variant — a ball entirely covered in streaked kernels (~4–5 cm),
  shown with a faceted-crystal bracelet (P0059). Likely a "corn pearl"/berry-ball style.

**Colorways** (each cob is unique; the folders are dominant-tone families):
White/Cream opal, Yellow-Gold, Peach/Orange (streaked), Amber/Brown, Light Pink, Berry Pink, Red/Burgundy,
Purple/Lavender, Blue-Gray (slate + cream), Black (onyx with pink accents), Multicolor (rainbow "glass gem").
Green & Silver folders contain essentially only stock photos — no real product.

## 7. Implications for the Three.js 3D hero

**Yes — procedurally modelable, and a good fit.**

- **Cob core:** capsule / `LatheGeometry` with slightly tapered rounded ends; length:diameter ≈ 2.3:1.
- **Kernels:** `InstancedMesh` of one squashed-sphere/rounded-box rondelle (~14 rows × ~20 rings ≈ 280 instances),
  placed on the surface along rows with small jitter, slight spiral twist, scale falloff toward the ends, normals facing out.
  End caps: concentric rings converge on the flat end (see Cap_Details P0028, P0185 for the pattern).
- **Material:** `MeshPhysicalMaterial` — `clearcoat: 1`, `clearcoatRoughness ~0.05–0.1`, `roughness ~0.35`,
  `transmission ~0.15–0.3` + `thickness` for the jade/opal translucency, light `sheen`/`iridescence` for the pearly ones.
  Per-instance color from the palette above (random weighted draw per colorway); swapping colorway = re-tinting instances (nice interaction).
- **Streak detail:** a small radial-streak texture (alpha/color map on the kernel, or a shader with radial noise) recreates the
  Peach/Amber starburst — this is the "premium" detail. Reference: P0048, P0070, P0208.
- **Husk:** the products **have no husk** — skip it or use a minimal stylized gold filigree cap instead. A husk would misrepresent the product.
- **Hardware:** gold cap = small `LatheGeometry` with scalloped profile + torus jump ring + simple lobster-clasp mesh, `metalness 1, roughness 0.25`, color `#C9A14A`.
- **Environment:** HDRI/`RoomEnvironment` for reflections is essential — the gloss is what sells it.
- **Alt hero:** the sphere variant (Round_Ornaments) is the same kernel system on a sphere — trivial to add.

**Reference photos for modeling/texturing:** P0070 (side profile + streaks), P0173 (full silhouette, row count),
P0028 & P0185 (end-on kernel spiral), P0206 & P0022 (multicolor distribution and gold cap), P0189 (cap/crown hardware),
P0059 (sphere). Real photos are not suitable as direct wrap textures (perspective, hands) — use them for color sampling and shape reference.
